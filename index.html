🔥 Heat Score Algorithm & Audit Rule (Max 100 Points)

Auditable Scoring Model
1. Recency (Max 40 pts)
• ≤30 days ago: 40 pts
• 31–60 days ago: 30 pts
• 61–90 days ago: 20 pts
• 91–180 days ago: 10 pts
• >180 days ago: 0 pts
2. Footprint Size (Max 25 pts)
• Mega (≥100,000 sq ft): 25 pts
• Large (50,000–99,999 sq ft): 20 pts
• Mid (20,000–49,999 sq ft): 15 pts
• Boutique (5,000–19,999 sq ft): 10 pts
• Compact (<5,000 sq ft): 5 pts
• Undisclosed size: 0 pts (never rewarded)
3. Deal Type (Max 20 pts)
• Relocation: 20 pts
• Expansion: 20 pts
• New lease: 20 pts
• Pre-commitment: 20 pts
• Renewal: 5 pts
• Unstated: 5 pts
4. Status Modifier
• Active Priority: +15 pts
• Watchlist: +5 pts
• Engaged in Sales: -20 pts (if present)
• Unassigned: 0 pts
Formula: Total Heat Score = Recency (0-40) + Size (0-25) + Type (5-20) + Status (+5 to +15) [Clamped 0–100]
